Legal notice and privacy

No cookies, no tracking, no advertising, no third-party content. The only data kept is what you choose to send through the feedback form.

Publisher

Automotive Spirit is a non-commercial website published by Paul Bligny.

Contact

Hosting

GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages), 88 Colin P Kelly Jr Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, United States.

Privacy

  • The site sets no cookies and uses no local storage: there is nothing to accept or refuse.
  • It contains no analytics, no advertising and no social widgets.
  • Every file (pages, styles, script, fonts, photos) is served by the site itself. A security policy built into every page forbids the browser to load anything from another site, and links to other sites are opened without telling them which page you came from.
  • The main script adds the menu, the catalog filters, the zoom of the group map and opens model sheets over the page by reading them from this same site. The search and the comparison read their lists of models from this same site too; what you type or pick stays in your browser (a comparison is kept only in the page's address, so you can share it). These scripts store and send nothing. A second script opens the feedback form and sends it only when you press Send.
  • Like any web host, GitHub may record technical data such as IP addresses to operate and protect its service. The site's publisher has no access to this data. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Feedback form

The Send feedback and Report an error buttons open a form to report a bug, wrong information or a suggestion. Only what you send is kept:

  • What is kept: the kind of report, the name or pseudonym you give (optional, leave it empty to stay anonymous), your message, the address of the page it is about and the date. Your IP address, your browser and any other information are not stored, and the form uses no cookie.
  • Why: to fix and improve the site, which is the publisher's legitimate interest (GDPR, article 6(1)(f)). Messages are never sold, shared or used for anything else. Please do not write personal information in your message.
  • How: every message is first sorted by an AI system (kind, duplicates, abusive content), then read by a person. Abusive messages are deleted. No decision about you is taken automatically.
  • Where and how long: messages are received by a Cloudflare Worker and stored in a database located in the European Union (Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, United States; see the Cloudflare privacy policy). A message is deleted once it has been dealt with, and in any case after 12 months.
  • Your rights: you can ask to see or delete your message at the contact address above. You can also complain to the CNIL (cnil.fr).
