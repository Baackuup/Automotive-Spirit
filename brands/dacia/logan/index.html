Dacia Logan

Dacia · Romania

Third generation (LJI; 2020) · 2020–present

Abstract

This generation of the Dacia Logan was built 2020–present in Romania: Mioveni 2020-2025, Morocco: Casablanca (Somaca). Body styles: 4-door saloon. Designed by Renault Technocentre & Renault Technologie Roumanie.
