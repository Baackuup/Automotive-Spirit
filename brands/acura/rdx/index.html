Acura RDX

Acura · Japan

Third generation (TC1/2; 2019) · 2018–2026

Abstract

This generation of the Acura RDX was built May 2018 – May 05, 2026 in United States: East Liberty, Ohio (East Liberty Auto Plant); Marysville, Ohio (Marysville Auto Plant, 2021-2022 PMC Edition), China: Guangzhou (GAC Honda, 2018-2022). Layout: Front engine, front-wheel drive / all-wheel drive (SH-AWD). Designed by Randall Smock.
