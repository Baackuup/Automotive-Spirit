Hyundai Grandeur

Hyundai · South Korea

Seventh generation (GN7; 2022) · 2022–present

Abstract

This generation of the Hyundai Grandeur was built November 2022 – present in South Korea: Asan (Hyundai Motor Asan Plant). Body styles: 4-door sedan. Layout: Front-engine, front-wheel-drive.
