Hyundai Xcient

Hyundai · South Korea

2013–present

Abstract

The Hyundai Xcient was built 2013–present in Jeonju, South Korea (Hyundai Motors Jeonju), Ziyang, China (Sichuan Hyundai), Kaliningrad, Russia (Avtotor) and other plants.
