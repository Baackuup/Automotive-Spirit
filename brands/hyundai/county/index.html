Hyundai County

Hyundai · South Korea

1998–present

Abstract

The Hyundai County is a minibus, built 1998–present in Vietnam: Ninh Bình (Hyundai Thanh Cong Vietnam (HTCV), Tracomeco and Thaco, Thailand: Bangkok (Yontarakit Industry). Body styles: Single-decker bus (short & long body). Layout: Front-engine, rear-wheel-drive. Designed by Hyundai Motor Company Design Center.
