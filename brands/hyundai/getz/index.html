Hyundai Getz

Hyundai · South Korea

2002–2011

Abstract

The Hyundai Getz is a supermini, built 2002–2011 (South Korea) in South Korea: Ulsan, India: Chennai (Hyundai Motor India), Venezuela: Barcelona (MMC Automotriz) and other plants. Body styles: 3-door hatchback, 5-door hatchback. Layout: Front-engine, front-wheel-drive.
