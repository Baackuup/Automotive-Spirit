Mahindra Bolero

Mahindra · India

2000–present

Abstract

The Mahindra Bolero is a mid-size SUV, built 2000–present in Nashik, Maharashtra, India, Haridwar, Uttarakhand, India, Montevideo, Uruguay (Nordex S.A.). Body styles: 5 door SUV. Layout: Front-engine, rear-wheel-drive. Designed by Shyam Kumar Alepalli- Styling, Dilip Bogawat / Sanjay Shrivastava & Ulhas Khupse -Design Raju Rasal - Proto.
