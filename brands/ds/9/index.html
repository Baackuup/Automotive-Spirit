DS 9

DS · France

2020–2023

Abstract

The DS 9 is an executive car (E), built 01/2020–12/2023 in China: Shenzhen (Baoneng Group). Body styles: 4-door sedan. Layout: Transverse Front-engine, front-wheel drive / four-wheel drive. Designed by Romain Saquet.
