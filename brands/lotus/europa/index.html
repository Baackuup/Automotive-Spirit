Lotus Europa

Lotus · United Kingdom

1966–2010

Abstract

The Lotus Europa is a sports car (S), built 1966–1975 in United Kingdom: Hethel, Norfolk, England. Body styles: 2-door coupe. Layout: Longitudinal, Rear mid-engine, rear-wheel drive. Designed by Ron Hickman, Russell Carr (Europa S).
