Lotus Elise

Lotus · United Kingdom

1996–2021

Abstract

The Lotus Elise is a sports car (S), built 1996–2021 in United Kingdom: Hethel, Norfolk, England, Malaysia: Shah Alam (Lotus Cars Malaysia: Series 1, 1997–2000). Body styles: 2-door 2-seat targa top roadster. Layout: Transverse mid-engine, rear-wheel-drive.
