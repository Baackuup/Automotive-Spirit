GMC Hummer EV

GMC · United States

2021–present

Abstract

The GMC Hummer EV is a full-size SUV, built November 2021 – present in United States: Detroit, Michigan (Detroit/Hamtramck Assembly). Body styles: 4-door pickup truck, 5-door SUV. Layout: Dual-motors, rear-wheel drive (Hummer EV2). Designed by Rick Scheer (director of design), Brett Goliff (interior), Scott Martin (UX).
