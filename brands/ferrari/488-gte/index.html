Ferrari 488 GTE

Ferrari · Italy

LM GTE (FIA WEC & ELMS)

Abstract

The Ferrari 488 GTE is a racing car built for LM GTE (FIA WEC & ELMS), GTLM (IMSA SCC) by Ferrari Competizioni GT. Debut: 2016 24 Hours of Daytona.
