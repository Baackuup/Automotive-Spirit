Austin Allegro

Austin · United Kingdom

1973–1982

Abstract

The Austin Allegro is a small family car (C), built 1973–1982 in United Kingdom: Longbridge, Birmingham (Longbridge plant), Belgium: Seneffe, Italy: Lambrate, Milan (Innocenti: 1974–1975) and other plants. Body styles: 2-door saloon, 3-door estate, 4-door saloon. Layout: FF layout. Designed by Harris Mann.
