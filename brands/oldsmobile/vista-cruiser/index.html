Oldsmobile Vista Cruiser

Oldsmobile · United States

Third generation (1973–1977) · 1973–1977

Abstract

This generation of the Oldsmobile Vista Cruiser was built 1973–1977 in United States:, Lansing, Michigan (Lansing Car Assembly), Arlington Assembly, Arlington, Texas and other plants.
