Oldsmobile Cutlass Calais

Oldsmobile · United States

1984–1991

Abstract

The Oldsmobile Cutlass Calais is a compact, built 1984-1991 in United States: Lansing, Michigan (Lansing Car Assembly). Body styles: 2-door coupe, 4-door sedan. Layout: Transverse front-engine, front-wheel drive.
