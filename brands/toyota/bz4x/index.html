Toyota bZ4X

Toyota · Japan

2022–present

Abstract

The Toyota bZ4X is a compact crossover SUV, built April 2022 – present in Japan: Toyota, Aichi (Motomachi plant), China: Guangzhou (GAC Toyota); Tianjin (FAW Toyota until 2023), Indonesia: Karawang, West Java (TMMIN, 2025–present). Body styles: 5-door SUV. Layout: Front-motor, front-wheel-drive. Designed by Katsuhiro Suzuki (Chief Designer), Hirotada Kobayashi, Nobuhiko Honda, Tsuyoshi Oba, Junya Ono, and Hiroyuki Tada.
