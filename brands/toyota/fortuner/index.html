Toyota Fortuner

Toyota · Japan

Second generation (AN150/AN160; 2015) · 2015–present

Abstract

This generation of the Toyota Fortuner was built July 2015–present in Argentina: Zárate (TASA), Cambodia: Phnom Penh (TTMC), Egypt: Cairo (AOI) and other plants. Designed by Satoshi Okamoto, Takanori Ito and Tokuharu Mino, Akbar Juniarto, Muhammad Arief, Prana Jiwandhana and Supriyatno (2019 TRD Sportivo), Heng-Pin Chang and Tien-Tzu Lin (2020 facelift).
