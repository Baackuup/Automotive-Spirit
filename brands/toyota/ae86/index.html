Toyota AE86

Toyota · Japan

1983–1987

Abstract

The Toyota AE86 is a sport compact (C), built May 1983– July 1987 in Japan: Toyota City (Takaoka plant), Japan: Higashi-Fuji plant, Susono, Shizuoka (Kanto Auto Works). Body styles: 2-door coupé, 2-door convertible (dealer optional), 3-door liftback. Layout: Front-engine, rear-wheel drive. Designed by Fumio Agetsuma.
