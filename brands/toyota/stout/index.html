Toyota Stout

Toyota · Japan

Third generation (K110; 1978–2000) · 1978–2000

Abstract

This generation of the Toyota Stout was built 1978–2000 in Tahara Plant, Toyota City, Aichi, Japan, Gifu Auto Body Co., Ltd (from 1983), Durban, South Africa (from 1979). Body styles: 2/4-door pickup truck.
