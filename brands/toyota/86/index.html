Toyota 86

Toyota · Japan

Second generation (ZN8/ZD8; 2021) · 2021–present

Abstract

This generation of the Toyota 86 was built July 2021 – present (BRZ). Designed by Koichi Matsumoto (project chief designer), Kosuke Kubo and Kazuhiko Ito (GR86 exterior), Kazuki Tanaka and Yuka Kanazawa (interior).
