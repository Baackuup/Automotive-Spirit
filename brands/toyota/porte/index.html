Toyota Porte

Toyota · Japan

Second generation (XP140; 2012) · 2012–2020

Abstract

This generation of the Toyota Porte was built July 2012 – December 2020 in Japan: Susono, Shizuoka (Toyota Motor East Japan). Body styles: 4-door hatchback.
