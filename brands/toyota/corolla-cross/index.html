Toyota Corolla Cross

Toyota · Japan

2020–present

Abstract

The Toyota Corolla Cross is a compact crossover SUV (C), built July 2020 – present in Japan: Toyota, Aichi (Takaoka plant 2021–2025); Kanegasaki, Iwate (Iwate plant 2025–present), Thailand: Chachoengsao (TMT), Taiwan: Zhongli (Kuozui) and other plants. Body styles: 5-door SUV. Layout: Front-engine, front-wheel-drive.
