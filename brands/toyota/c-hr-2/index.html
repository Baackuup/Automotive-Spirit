Toyota C-HR+

Toyota · Japan

2025–present

Abstract

The Toyota C-HR+ is a compact crossover SUV (C), built September 2025 – present in Japan: Toyota City, Aichi (Takaoka plant). Body styles: 5-door coupe SUV. Layout: Front-motor, front-wheel-drive.
