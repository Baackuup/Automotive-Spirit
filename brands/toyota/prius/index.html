Toyota Prius

Toyota · Japan

Fourth generation (XW50; 2015) · 2015–2022

Abstract

This generation of the Toyota Prius is a compact car (C), built November 2015 – 2022 in Japan: Toyota, Aichi (Tsutsumi plant). Body styles: 5-door liftback. Layout: Front-engine, front-wheel-drive. Designed by Shunsaku Kodama (2012). This generation has its own article: Toyota Prius (XW50).
