Toyota Century

Toyota · Japan

Third generation (G60; 2018) · 2018–present

Abstract

This generation of the Toyota Century was built June 2018 – present in Japan: Susono, Shizuoka (Toyota Motor East Japan), Toyota City (Motomachi plant; from 2020). Body styles: 4-door limousine, 4-door convertible (Royal Parade Car). Designed by Masato Tanabe.
