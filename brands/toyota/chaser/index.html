Toyota Chaser

Toyota · Japan

Sixth generation (X100; 1996–2001) · 1996–2001

Abstract

This generation of the Toyota Chaser was built 1996–2001 in Miyawaka, Fukuoka (Miyata), (Toyota Motor Kyushu). Body styles: 4-door pillared hardtop sedan. Layout: Front engine, rear-wheel drive. Designed by Yukihiko Yaguchi.
