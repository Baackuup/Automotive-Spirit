Toyota Land Cruiser

Toyota · Japan

Land Cruiser FJ · 2026–present

Abstract

This generation of the Toyota Land Cruiser is a compact SUV, built February 2026 – present in Thailand: Ban Pho (TMT). Body styles: 5-door SUV. Layout: Front-engine, part-time four-wheel-drive. Designed by Koji Inoue (project chief designer), Lu Yi, Kosuke Kubo and Kohei Toyoda (exterior), Yuki Ujita (interior). This generation has its own article: Toyota Land Cruiser FJ.
