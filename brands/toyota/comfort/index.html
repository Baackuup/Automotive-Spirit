Toyota Comfort

Toyota · Japan

1995–2018

Abstract

The Toyota Comfort is a mid-size car (D), built December 1995–January 2018 in Japan: Susono, Shizuoka (Higashi Fuji plant). Body styles: 4-door notchback sedan. Layout: Front-engine, rear-wheel-drive.
