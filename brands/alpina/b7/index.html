Alpina B7

Alpina · Germany

Fifth generation (G12) · 2016–2022

Abstract

This generation of the Alpina B7 is a full-size luxury car (F), built 2016–2022 in Germany:, Dingolfing (initial assembly), Buchloe (final assembly). Body styles: 4-door (LWB) sedan. Layout: Front-engine, rear-wheel-drive. This generation has its own article: Alpina B7 (G12).
