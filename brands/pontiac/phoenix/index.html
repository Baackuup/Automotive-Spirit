Pontiac Phoenix

Pontiac · United States

Second generation (1980–1984) · 1979–1984

Abstract

This generation of the Pontiac Phoenix was built 1979–1984 in Oklahoma City Assembly, Oklahoma City, Oklahoma, North Tarrytown Assembly, North Tarrytown, New York. Body styles: 2-door coupe, 5-door hatchback. Layout: Transverse front-engine, front-wheel drive.
