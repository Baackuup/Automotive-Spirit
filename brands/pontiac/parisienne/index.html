Pontiac Parisienne

Pontiac · United States

Fifth generation: 1977–1986 · 1977–1986

Abstract

This generation of the Pontiac Parisienne was built 1977–1986 in Oshawa, Ontario, Canada, Kansas City, Kansas, United States (Fairfax Assembly). Body styles: 2-door coupe, 4-door sedan, 4-door station wagon.
