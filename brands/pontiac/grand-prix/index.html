Pontiac Grand Prix

Pontiac · United States

Eighth generation (2004–2008) · 2003–2008

Abstract

This generation of the Pontiac Grand Prix is a mid-size car, built January 2, 2003 – April 24, 2008 in Oshawa, Ontario, Canada (Oshawa Car Assembly). Body styles: 4-door sedan. Layout: Transverse front-engine, front-wheel drive. Designed by John Manoogian II (2000).
