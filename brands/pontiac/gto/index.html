Pontiac GTO

Pontiac · United States

Fifth generation · 2003–2006

Abstract

This generation of the Pontiac GTO was built Late 2003–June 14, 2006 in Australia: Adelaide, South Australia (Elizabeth). Body styles: 2-door coupé. Designed by Tony Stolfo.
