Pontiac LeMans

Pontiac · United States

Sixth generation (1988–1993) · 1987–1993

Abstract

This generation of the Pontiac LeMans is a subcompact, built 1987–1993 in South Korea: Bupyeong, Incheon. Body styles: 3-door hatchback, 5-door hatchback (NZ), 4-door sedan. Layout: Front-engine, front-wheel-drive.
