Pontiac Firebird

Pontiac · United States

Fourth generation (1993–2002) · 1992–2002

Abstract

This generation of the Pontiac Firebird was built November 1992 – August 30, 2002 in Canada: Sainte-Thérèse, Quebec (Sainte-Thérèse Assembly). Body styles: 2-door liftback, 2-door convertible. Designed by John Folden.
