Hongqi LS7

Hongqi · China

2022–present

Abstract

The Hongqi LS7 is a full-size luxury SUV (F), built 2022–present in China: Changchun, Jilin. Body styles: 5-door SUV. Layout: Front-engine, four-wheel-drive. Designed by Giles Taylor.
