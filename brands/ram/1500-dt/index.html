Ram 1500 (DT)

Ram · United States

2018–present

Abstract

The Ram 1500 (DT) was built 2018–present in United States: Sterling Heights, Michigan (Sterling Heights Assembly), Mexico: Saltillo (Saltillo Truck Assembly). Body styles: 4-door extended cab ("Quad Cab"), 4-door crew cab.
