Ram 1500 REV

Ram · United States

2024–2027

Abstract

The Ram 1500 REV is a full-size pickup truck, built April 2027 (to commence; originally 2024) in United States: Sterling Heights, Michigan (Sterling Heights Assembly). Body styles: 4-door crew cab. Layout: Front-engine, dual-motor, all-wheel-drive.
