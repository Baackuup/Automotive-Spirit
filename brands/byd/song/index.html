BYD Song

BYD · China

Song Ultra (2026) · 2026–present

Abstract

This generation of the BYD Song is a mid-size SUV (D-segment), built 2026–present in China: Zhengzhou, Henan; Changsha, Hunan. Body styles: 5-door crossover SUV. Layout: Rear-motor, rear-wheel-drive. Designed by Under the lead of Wolfgang Egger. This generation has its own article: BYD Song Ultra.
