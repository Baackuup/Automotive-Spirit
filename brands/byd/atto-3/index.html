BYD Atto 3

BYD · China

2022–present

Abstract

The BYD Atto 3 is a compact SUV (C-segment), built January 2022 – present in China: Changsha, Hunan; Changzhou, Jiangsu; Hefei, Anhui, India: Sriperumbudur, Tamil Nadu (BYD India, 2022–present), Thailand: Rayong (BYD Auto (Thailand), 2024–present). Body styles: 5-door crossover SUV. Layout: Front-motor, front-wheel-drive. Designed by Under the lead of Wolfgang Egger.
