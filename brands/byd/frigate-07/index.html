BYD Frigate 07

BYD · China

2022–2025

Abstract

The BYD Frigate 07 is a mid-size SUV, built October 2022 – 2025 in China: Xi'an, Shaanxi. Body styles: 5-door crossover SUV. Layout: Front-engine, front-wheel-drive. Designed by Under the lead of Wolfgang Egger.
