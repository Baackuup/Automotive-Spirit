SEAT Ibiza

SEAT · Spain

Fifth generation (6F/KJ1; 2017) · 2017–present

Abstract

This generation of the SEAT Ibiza was built 2017–present in Spain: Martorell, Catalonia, Algeria: Relizane. Body styles: 5-door hatchback. Designed by Julio Lozano.
