Buick LeSabre

Buick · United States

Eighth generation (2000–2005) · 1999–2005

Abstract

This generation of the Buick LeSabre was built March 1999 - August 2005 in Lake Orion, Michigan, United States (Orion Assembly), Hamtramck, Michigan, United States (Detroit/Hamtramck Assembly). Body styles: 4-door sedan. Layout: Transverse front-engine, front-wheel drive.
