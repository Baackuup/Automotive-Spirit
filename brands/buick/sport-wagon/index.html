Buick Sport Wagon

Buick · United States

1964–1971

Abstract

The Buick Sport Wagon is a mid-size, built 1964-1971 in Buick City, Flint, Michigan, Fremont Assembly, Fremont, California, Leeds Assembly, Leeds, Kansas City, Missouri. Body styles: 4-door station wagon. Layout: FR layout.
