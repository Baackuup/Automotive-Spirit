Rolls-Royce Phantom VI

Rolls-Royce · United Kingdom

1968–1990

Abstract

The Rolls-Royce Phantom VI was built 1968–1990 in United Kingdom: West Sussex, England. Body styles: Limousines and other styles to buyer's choice. Layout: FR layout.
