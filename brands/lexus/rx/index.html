Lexus RX

Lexus · Japan

Fifth generation (ALA10/ALH10; 2022) · 2022–present

Abstract

This generation of the Lexus RX was built October 2022 – present in Japan: Miyawaka, Fukuoka (TMK), Canada: Cambridge, Ontario (TMMC). Designed by Jōta Kusakari.
