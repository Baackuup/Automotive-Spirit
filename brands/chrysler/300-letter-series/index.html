Chrysler 300 letter series

Chrysler · United States

1955–1970

Abstract

The Chrysler 300 letter series is a personal luxury car, built 1955–1965 in United States: Detroit, Michigan (Jefferson Avenue Assembly). Layout: Front-engine, rear-wheel-drive.
