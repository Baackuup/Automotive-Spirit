Chrysler Cirrus

Chrysler · United States

1994–2000

Abstract

The Chrysler Cirrus is a mid-size car, built June 1994–2000 in United States: Sterling Heights, Michigan (Sterling Heights Assembly). Body styles: 4-door notchback sedan. Layout: Transverse front-engine, front-wheel drive. Designed by Michael Santoro (1991).
