Chrysler Sebring

Chrysler · United States

Third generation (JS; 2007) · 2006–2010

Abstract

This generation of the Chrysler Sebring was built 2006–2010 in Sterling Heights, Michigan, United States, Beijing, China (Beijing Benz). Body styles: 4-door sedan, 2-door convertible. Layout: Front-engine, front-wheel drive / all-wheel drive.
