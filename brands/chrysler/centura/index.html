Chrysler Centura

Chrysler · United States

1975–1978

Abstract

The Chrysler Centura is a mid-size car, built March 1975–1978 in Australia: Tonsley Park, 19,770 produced. Body styles: 4-door sedan. Layout: Front-engine, rear-wheel drive.
