Chrysler Alpine

Chrysler · United States

1975–1986

Abstract

The Chrysler Alpine is a large family car (D), built 1975–1986 in France: Poissy (Poissy plant), United Kingdom: Ryton-on-Dunsmore (Ryton plant), Spain: Madrid (Chrysler España) and other plants. Layout: FF layout. Designed by Roy Axe.
