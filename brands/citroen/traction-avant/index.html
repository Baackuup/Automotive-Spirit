Citroën Traction Avant

Citroën · France

1934–1957

Abstract

The Citroën Traction Avant is an executive car (E), built 7: 1934–1941 in France: Paris, Belgium: Forest, Denmark: Copenhagen and other plants. Body styles: 4-door saloon, 2-door saloon, 2-door convertible, 5-door hatchback. Layout: FMF layout. Designed by Flaminio Bertoni, André Lefèbvre.
