Citroën C3 Picasso

Citroën · France

2008–2017

Abstract

The Citroën C3 Picasso is a mini MPV (M), built 2008–2017 in Slovakia: Trnava (Trnava Plant), Spain: Zaragoza (General Motors), Brazil: Porto Real (Citroën Brazil). Body styles: 5-door hatchback. Layout: Front-engine, front-wheel-drive. Designed by Frédéric Duvernier (exterior), Miles Nurnberger (exterior), Andreas Stump (interior).
