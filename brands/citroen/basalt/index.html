Citroën Basalt

Citroën · France

2024–present

Abstract

The Citroën Basalt is a subcompact crossover SUV, built 2024–present in India: Tiruvallur (Citroën India), Brazil: Porto Real (Stellantis do Brasil). Body styles: 5-door coupe SUV. Layout: Front-engine, front-wheel-drive. Designed by Antoine Gaillot-Drevon.
