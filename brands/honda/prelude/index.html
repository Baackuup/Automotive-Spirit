Honda Prelude

Honda · Japan

Sixth generation (BF1; 2025) · 2025–present

Abstract

This generation of the Honda Prelude was built August 2025 – present. Body styles: 3-door liftback coupe. Designed by Norihito Ōnuma (exterior).
