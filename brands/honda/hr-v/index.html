Honda HR-V

Honda · Japan

Third generation (2021) · 2021–present

Abstract

This generation of the Honda HR-V is a subcompact crossover SUV (B), built April 2021 – present in Japan: Yorii, Saitama, China: Guangzhou (GAC Honda); Wuhan (Dongfeng Honda), Taiwan: Pingtung and other plants. Body styles: 5-door SUV. Layout: Front-engine, front-wheel-drive. Designed by Daisuke Akojima.
