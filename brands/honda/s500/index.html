Honda S500

Honda · Japan

1963–1964

Abstract

The Honda S500 is a sports car, built 1963–1964 in Hamamatsu factory, Shizuoka Prefecture, Japan. Body styles: 2-door roadster. Layout: Front-engine, rear-wheel-drive layout.
