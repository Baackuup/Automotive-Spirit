Honda Fit

Honda · Japan

Fourth generation (GR/GS; 2020) · 2020–present

Abstract

This generation of the Honda Fit was built February 2020 – present in Japan: Suzuka, Mie, China: Guangzhou (GAC Honda, 2020–2026); Wuhan (Dongfeng Honda, Life, 2020–2025), Taiwan: Pingtung. Designed by Baek Jongkuk.
