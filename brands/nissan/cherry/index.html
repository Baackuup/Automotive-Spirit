Nissan Cherry

Nissan · Japan

Fourth generation (N12; 1982) · 1982–1986

Abstract

This generation of the Nissan Cherry was built 1982–1986 in Japan: Oppama Plant, Yokosuka, Kanagawa, Greece: Volos (TEOKAR), Italy: Pratola Serra. Body styles: 3-door hatchback, 4-door sedan, 5-door hatchback.
