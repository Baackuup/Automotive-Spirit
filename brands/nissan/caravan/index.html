Nissan Caravan

Nissan · Japan

Fifth generation (E26; 2012) · 2012–present

Abstract

This generation of the Nissan Caravan was built June 2012 – present in Japan: Kanda, Fukuoka, Kyushu (Nissan Shatai), Thailand: Samut Prakan (NMT). Designed by Koichi Kuraoka (chief designer).
