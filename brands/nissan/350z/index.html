Nissan 350Z

Nissan · Japan

2002–2008

Abstract

The Nissan 350Z is a sports car (S), built July 2002–2008 (Coupe) in Japan: Oppama (2002–2003), Japan: Kaminokawa, Tochigi (2003–2008). Body styles: 2-door hatchback coupé, 2-door roadster. Layout: Front mid-engine, rear-wheel-drive. Designed by Ajay Panchal at Nissan Design America (2000).
