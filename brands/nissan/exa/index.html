Nissan · Japan

Nissan EXA (N13; 1986–1990) · 1986–1990

Abstract

This generation of the Nissan EXA was built 1986–1990 in Japan. Body styles: 3-door T-top coupé. Layout: Transverse front-engine, front-wheel drive. Designed by Koichiro Kawamura.
