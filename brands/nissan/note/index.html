Nissan Note

Nissan · Japan

Third generation (E13; 2020) · 2020–present

Abstract

This generation of the Nissan Note was built December 2020 – present in Japan: Yokosuka, Kanagawa (Oppama Plant). Layout: Front-engine, front-motor, front-wheel-drive. Designed by Giovanny Arroba, Kazunori Murabayashi (Note Aura).
