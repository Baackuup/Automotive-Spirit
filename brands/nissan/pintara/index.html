Nissan Pintara

Nissan · Japan

Second generation (U12; 1989–1992) · 1989–1992

Abstract

This generation of the Nissan Pintara was built 1989–1992. Body styles: 4-door sedan, 5-door hatchback (Superhatch). Layout: Front-engine, front-wheel-drive.
