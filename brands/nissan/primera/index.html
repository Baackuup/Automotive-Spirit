Nissan Primera

Nissan · Japan

Third generation (P12; 2001) · 2001–2008

Abstract

This generation of the Nissan Primera was built 2001–2008 in Japan: Kyushu, United Kingdom: Sunderland (NMUK). Body styles: 4-door saloon, 5-door liftback, 5-door estate. Designed by Stéphane Schwarz (1999).
