Nissan Titan

Nissan · Japan

Second generation (A61; 2016) · 2015–2024

Abstract

This generation of the Nissan Titan is a full-size pickup truck, built November 2015–November 2024. Body styles: 2-door regular cab (2016–2019), 4-door extended cab, 4-door crew cab. Designed by Randy Rodriguez (exterior: 2012), Diane Allen (2012, 2013), Fred Diaz (executive consultant: 2013).
