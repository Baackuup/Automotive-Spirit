Nissan Lucino

Nissan · Japan

1994–2000

Abstract

The Nissan Lucino is a sport compact (coupé), built 1994–1999 (coupé; until 2000 in Mexico) in Japan: Yokosuka, Kanagawa (Oppama plant), United States: Smyrna, Tennessee (Nissan North America; coupé only), Mexico: Cuernavaca, Morelos (Nissan Mexicana; coupé only). Body styles: 2-door coupé, 3-door hatchback, 5-door hatchback. Layout: Front-engine, front-wheel-drive.
