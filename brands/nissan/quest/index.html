Nissan Quest

Nissan · Japan

Fourth generation (RE52; 2011) · 2010–2017

Abstract

This generation of the Nissan Quest was built December 2010 – 2017 in Japan: Kanda, Fukuoka (Nissan Shatai). Body styles: 5-door minivan.
