Nissan Serena

Nissan · Japan

Sixth generation (C28; 2022) · 2022–present

Abstract

This generation of the Nissan Serena was built November 2022 – present in Japan: Kanda, Fukuoka (Nissan Motor Kyushu), Malaysia: Serendah (TCMA). Layout: Front-engine, front-wheel-drive.
