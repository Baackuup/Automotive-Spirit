Nissan Cedric

Nissan · Japan

Tenth generation (Y34; 1999) · 1999–2004

Abstract

This generation of the Nissan Cedric was built 1999–2004 in Japan: Kaminokawa, Tochigi. Body styles: 4-door hardtop. Layout: FR layout/AWD.
