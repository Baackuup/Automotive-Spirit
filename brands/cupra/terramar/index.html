Cupra Terramar

Cupra · Spain

2024–present

Abstract

The Cupra Terramar is a compact crossover SUV (C), built September 2024–present in Hungary: Győr (Audi Hungaria). Body styles: 5-door SUV. Layout: Front-engine, front-wheel-drive. Designed by Jorge Díez (Design Director, Cupra).
