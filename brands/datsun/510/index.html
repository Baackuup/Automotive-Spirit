Datsun 510

Datsun · Japan

1967–1973

Abstract

The Datsun 510 was built August 1967–1973 in Japan: Yokosuka, Kanagawa (Oppama plant), Australia: Melbourne, Victoria, New Zealand: Thames and other plants. Body styles: 2/4-door sedan, 2-door coupé, 5-door station wagon. Layout: FR layout. Designed by Teruo Uchino.
