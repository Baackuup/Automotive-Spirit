Fiat Tipo

Fiat · Italy

Fiat Tipo (2015) · 2015–2026

Abstract

This generation of the Fiat Tipo is a compact car (C), built 2015–2026 in Turkey: Bursa (Tofaş). Body styles: 4-door sedan, 5-door hatchback, 5-door cross hatchback, 5-door station wagon, 5-door cross station wagon. Layout: Front-engine, front-wheel-drive. Designed by Roberto Giolito at Centro Stile Fiat.
