Fiat 1300 and 1500

Fiat · Italy

1961–1977

Abstract

The Fiat 1300 and 1500 is a large family car (D), built 1961–1972 in Italy: Turin, Argentina: Córdoba, Colombia: Bogotá (CCA) and other plants. Body styles: 4-door saloon, 5-door estate, 2-door cabriolet, 2-door coupé. Layout: FR layout. Designed by Mario Boano at Centro Stile Fiat.
