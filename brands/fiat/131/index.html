Fiat 131

Fiat · Italy

1974–2010

Abstract

The Fiat 131 is a family car, built 1974–1984 in Italy: Comprensorio di Mirafiori, Turin, Colombia: Bogotá (CCA), Indonesia: Jakarta and other plants. Body styles: 4-door saloon, 2-door saloon, 5-door estate, 3-door panel van. Layout: Front-engine, rear-wheel-drive.
