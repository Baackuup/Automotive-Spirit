Fiat Dino

Fiat · Italy

1966–1972

Abstract

The Fiat Dino is a sports car (S), built 1966–1972 in Italy: Rivalta, Turin (1966–1969), Italy: Maranello, Modena (1969–1972). Body styles: 2-door coupé, 2-door spider. Layout: Front-engine, rear-wheel-drive. Designed by Spider:, Filippo Sapino at Pininfarina, Coupé:.
