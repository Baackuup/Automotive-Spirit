Lamborghini Huracán

Lamborghini · Italy

2014–2024

Abstract

The Lamborghini Huracán is a sports car (S), built 2014–2024 in Italy: Sant'Agata Bolognese. Body styles: 2-door coupé, 2-door convertible. Layout: Longitudinal, mid-engine, four-wheel-drive (LP 610-4, LP 640-4 Performante, LP 640-4 Sterrato). Designed by Filippo Perini (original design), Mitja Borkert (LP 640-4, facelift).
