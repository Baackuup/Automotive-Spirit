Volvo V70

Volvo · Sweden

Third generation (2007–2016) · 2006–2016

Abstract

This generation of the Volvo V70 was built December 2006–2016 in Sweden: Torslanda (Torslandaverken). Designed by Stefan Jansson (exterior), Jonathan Disley (interior).
