Volvo C30

Volvo · Sweden

2006–2013

Abstract

The Volvo C30 is a premium compact/Subcompact executive car (C), built 2006–2013 in Belgium: Ghent (VCG). Body styles: 3-door hatchback. Layout: Front-engine, front-wheel-drive. Designed by Simon Lamarre.
