Volvo V60

Volvo · Sweden

Second generation (2018) · 2018–present

Abstract

This generation of the Volvo V60 was built February 2018–present in Sweden: Torslanda (Torslandaverken), Belgium: Ghent (Volvo Car Gent), Malaysia: Shah Alam (VCMM). Layout: Front engine. Designed by T. Jon Mayer, Maxime Prevoteaux.
