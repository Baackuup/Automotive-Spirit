Volvo S40

Volvo · Sweden

Second generation (2003–2012) · 2003–2012

Abstract

This generation of the Volvo S40 was built 2003–2012 in Belgium: Ghent (VCG), China: Chongqing (CFMA), Malaysia: Shah Alam (SMA) and other plants. Body styles: 4-door saloon. Layout: Front-engine, front-wheel-drive or four-wheel-drive. Designed by Fedde Talsma.
