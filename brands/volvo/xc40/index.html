Volvo XC40

Volvo · Sweden

2017–present

Abstract

The Volvo XC40 is a subcompact crossover SUV (C), built November 2017 – present in Belgium: Ghent (VCG), China: Luqiao, Zhejiang (Luqiao CMA Super Factory), Malaysia: Shah Alam (VCMM) and other plants. Body styles: 5-door SUV, 5-door coupé SUV (C40/EC40). Layout: Front-engine, front-wheel-drive. Designed by Ian Kettle, Thomas Ingenlath, Katharina Sachs (C40/EC40).
