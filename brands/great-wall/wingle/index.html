Great Wall Wingle

Great Wall (GWM) · China

2006–present

Abstract

The Great Wall Wingle is a full-size pickup truck, built 2006–present in China: Baoding (GWM), Bulgaria: Bahovitsa (Litex Motors), Iran: Golpayegan (Diar Automobile Company) and other plants. Body styles: 4-door crew cab (2006–Present), 2-door pickup truck (2006–2021).
