Porsche 959

Porsche · Germany

1986–1993

Abstract

The Porsche 959 is a sports car (S), built 1986–1993 in Germany: Stuttgart (Karosserie Baur). Body styles: 2-door coupé. Layout: Rear-engine, four-wheel-drive. Designed by Helmuth Bott.
