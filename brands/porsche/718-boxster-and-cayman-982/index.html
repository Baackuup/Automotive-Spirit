Porsche 718 Boxster and Cayman (982)

Porsche · Germany

2016–2025

Abstract

The Porsche 718 Boxster and Cayman (982) is a sports car (S), built 2016–2025 in Germany: Stuttgart, Germany: Osnabrück. Body styles: 2-door roadster (718 Boxster / Spyder), 2-door fastback coupé (718 Cayman). Layout: MR layout. Designed by Peter Varga (2015), Fabian Schmölz (GT4, 2018).
