Porsche 911 RSR (2017)

Porsche · Germany

GT Endurance (IMSA WeatherTech SportsCar GTLM and FIA WEC GTE Pro and GTE Am)

Abstract

The Porsche 911 RSR (2017) is a racing car built for GT Endurance (IMSA WeatherTech SportsCar GTLM and FIA WEC GTE Pro and GTE Am) by Porsche. Debut: 2017 24 Hours of Daytona (IMSA SportsCar Championship).

The Porsche 911 RSR (2017) shares its generations with the Porsche 911 RSR (2017): those listed are of that common design.
