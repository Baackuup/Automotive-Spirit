Isuzu MU

Isuzu · Japan

Second generation (1998) · 1998–2005

Abstract

This generation of the Isuzu MU was built 1998–2005 in Japan: Fujisawa, Kanagawa (Fujisawa Plant), United States: Lafayette, Indiana., United Kingdom: Luton (IBC Vehicles) and other plants. Designed by Takahiro Uematsu (1996).
