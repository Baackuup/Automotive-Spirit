Isuzu Ascender

Isuzu · Japan

2002–2008

Abstract

The Isuzu Ascender is a mid-size SUV, built 2002–June 2008 in United States:, Moraine, Ohio (Moraine Assembly), Oklahoma City, Oklahoma (Oklahoma City Assembly). Body styles: 5-door SUV. Layout: Front-engine, rear-wheel drive / four-wheel drive.
