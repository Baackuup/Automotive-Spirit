Holden Astra

Holden · Australia

Seventh generation (BK, BL; 2016) · 2016–2020

Abstract

This generation of the Holden Astra was built 2016–2020 in Poland: Gliwice (hatchback), South Korea: Gunsan, Jeonbuk (sedan), Ellesmere Port, United Kingdom (Vauxhall Ellesmere Port) (wagon). Body styles: 4-door sedan, 5-door hatchback, 5-door station wagon. Designed by Jeasoo Kim (hatchback and station wagons), Stuart Cooper (sedan).
