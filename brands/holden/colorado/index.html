Holden Colorado

Holden · Australia

2008–2020

Abstract

The Holden Colorado is a compact pickup truck (Mk1), built 2008–2020 in Thailand: Rayong (General Motors Thailand). Body styles: 2-door pickup truck, 4-door pickup truck. Layout: Front-engine, rear-wheel-drive.
