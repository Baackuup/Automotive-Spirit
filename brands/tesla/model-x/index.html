Tesla Model X

Tesla · United States

2015–2026

Abstract

The Tesla Model X is a mid-size luxury crossover SUV (E), built 2015 – May 2026 in United States: Fremont, California (Fremont Factory), Netherlands: Tilburg (Tilburg final assembly, 2016–2021). Body styles: 5-door SUV. Layout: Dual-motor, all-wheel-drive. Designed by Franz von Holzhausen.
