Renault Fuego

Renault · France

1980–1992

Abstract

The Renault Fuego is a sport compact, built February 1980–1986 (Europe) in France: Boulogne-Billancourt (RSAS: 1980–1985), France: Maubeuge (MCA: 1979–1985), Argentina: Santa Isabel (Renault Argentina: 1982–1992) and other plants. Body styles: 3-door liftback. Layout: FF layout. Designed by Michel Jardin, Francois Lampreia, and Robert Opron.
