Renault Talisman

Renault · France

2015–2025

Abstract

The Renault Talisman is a mid-size car / large family car (D-segment), built 2015–2022 (France) in France: Douai (Douai Renault Factory), South Korea: Busan (Renault Korea Motors). Body styles: 4-door saloon, 5-door estate. Layout: Front-engine, front-wheel-drive. Designed by Alexis Martot, Under the lead of Laurens van den Acker.
