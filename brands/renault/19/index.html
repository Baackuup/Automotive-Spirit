Renault 19

Renault · France

1988–2000

Abstract

The Renault 19 is a small family car (C), built 1988–1996 in France: Douai, France: Maubeuge, Belgium: Vilvoorde and other plants. Body styles: 3/5-door hatchback, 4-door saloon ("Chamade"), 2-door convertible. Layout: FF layout. Designed by Giorgetto Giugiaro at Italdesign.
