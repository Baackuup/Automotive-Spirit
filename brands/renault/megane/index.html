Renault Mégane

Renault · France

Fifth generation (Megane E-Tech, 2022) · 2022–present

Abstract

This generation of the Renault Mégane is a small family car, built 2022–present in France: Douai (Douai Renault Factory). Body styles: 5-door hatchback. Layout: Front-motor, front-wheel-drive.
