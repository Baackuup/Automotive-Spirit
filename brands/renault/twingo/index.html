Renault Twingo

Renault · France

Fourth generation (Twingo E-Tech, 2026) · 2026–present

Abstract

This generation of the Renault Twingo is a city car (A), built 2026–present in Slovenia: Novo Mesto (Renault Slovenia). Body styles: 5-door hatchback. Layout: Front-motor, front-wheel-drive. Designed by Gilles Vidal (design director).

Figures of the current version from the manufacturer: Renault Twingo E-Tech électrique specifications (renault.fr), 2026-11.
