Renault Scénic

Renault · France

Fifth generation (Scenic E-Tech, 2024) · 2024–present

Abstract

This generation of the Renault Scénic is a compact crossover SUV (C), built 2024–present in France: Douai (Douai Renault Factory). Body styles: 5-door SUV. Layout: Front-motor, front-wheel-drive. Designed by Gilles Vidal.

Figures of the current version from the manufacturer: Renault Scenic E-Tech électrique specifications (renault.fr), 2026-11.
