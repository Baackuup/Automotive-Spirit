Renault Captur

Renault · France

Second generation (JB/JE; 2019) · 2019–present

Abstract

This generation of the Renault Captur was built 2019–present in Spain: Valladolid (Renault Spain), China: Wuhan (Dongfeng Renault until 2020). Layout: Front-engine, front-wheel-drive. Designed by Pierre Sabas (exterior; 2018), Under the lead of Laurens van den Acker.

Figures of the current version from the manufacturer: Renault Captur specifications (renault.fr), 2026-11.
