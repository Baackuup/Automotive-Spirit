Renault Dauphine

Renault · France

1956–1970

Abstract

The Renault Dauphine is a small family car or economy car, built 1956–1967 in France: Flins (Flins Factory), Algeria: El-Harrach (CARAL), Argentina: Santa Isabel (IKA) and other plants. Body styles: 4-door saloon. Layout: RR layout. Designed by Fernand Picard, Robert Barthaud, Jacques Ousset.
