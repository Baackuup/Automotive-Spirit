Renault Express

Renault · France

Renault Express (2020) · 2021–present

Abstract

This generation of the Renault Express is a leisure activity vehicle (Express), built 2021–present in Morocco: Tangier (Renault Med). Body styles: 5-door multi-purpose vehicle, 4/5-door panel van. Layout: Front-engine, front-wheel-drive. Designed by Under the lead of Laurens van den Acker.
