Lincoln Versailles

Lincoln · United States

1977–1980

Abstract

The Lincoln Versailles is a compact luxury car, built 1977–1980 in United States: Wayne, Michigan (Wayne Stamping & Assembly), United States: Mahwah, New Jersey (Mahwah Assembly). Body styles: 4-door sedan. Layout: FR layout.
