Lincoln Town Car

Lincoln · United States

Third generation (FN145; 1998–2011) · 1997–2011

Abstract

This generation of the Lincoln Town Car was built October 1997 – August 29, 2011 in United States: Wixom, Michigan (1998–2007), Canada: St. Thomas, Ontario (2008–2011), China: Changchun (1998–2005). Body styles: 4-door sedan. Designed by Fritz Mayhew (1994).
