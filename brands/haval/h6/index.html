Haval H6

Haval · China

Third generation (2020) · 2020–present

Abstract

This generation of the Haval H6 was built 2020–present in China: Tianjin, Thailand: Rayong (GWM Thailand), Malaysia: Pegoh, Malacca (EPMB) and other plants. Body styles: 5-door SUV, 5-door coupe SUV (H6S / H6 GT). Designed by Under the lead of Phil Stilo.
