Daihatsu Fellow Max

Daihatsu · Japan

Daihatsu Max (2001) · 2001–2005

Abstract

This generation of the Daihatsu Fellow Max is a kei car, built November 2001 – December 2005 in Ikeda, Osaka, Japan. Body styles: 5-door hatchback. Layout: Front engine, front-wheel-drive or four-wheel-drive.
