Daihatsu Zebra

Daihatsu · Japan

Second generation (S90/S91/S92; 1995) · 1995–2007

Abstract

This generation of the Daihatsu Zebra was built April 1995 – October 2007 in Indonesia: Sunter, Jakarta (ADM - Sunter Assembly Plant), Malaysia: Rawang, Selangor (Perodua Manufacturing Sdn. Bhd.), China: Liuzhou, Guangxi (SAIC-GM-Wuling).
