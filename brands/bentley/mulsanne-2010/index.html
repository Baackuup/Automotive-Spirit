Bentley Mulsanne

Bentley · United Kingdom

Bentley Mulsanne (2010) · 2010–2020

Abstract

This generation of the Bentley Mulsanne is a full-size luxury car (F), built March 2010 – June 2020 in United Kingdom: Crewe (Bentley Crewe). Body styles: 4-door saloon. Layout: Front-engine, rear-wheel-drive. Designed by Dirk van Braeckel (2007), Robin Page (interior).
