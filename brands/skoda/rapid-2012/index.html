Škoda Rapid

Škoda · Czech Republic

Škoda Rapid (2012) · 2012–2023

Abstract

This generation of the Škoda Rapid is a small family car, built 2012–2019 (Czech Republic) in Czech Republic: Mladá Boleslav, China: Yizheng (SAIC-VW), Russia: Kaluga (VW Group Rus) and other plants. Body styles: 4-door sedan (China), 5-door liftback, 5-door hatchback. Layout: Front-engine, front-wheel-drive.
