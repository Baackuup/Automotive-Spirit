Škoda Felicia

Škoda · Czech Republic

Škoda Felicia (1994–2001) · 1994–2001

Abstract

The Škoda Felicia is a supermini (B), built 1994–2001 (Czech Republic, Poland) in Vrchlabí, Czech Republic, Mladá Boleslav, Czech Republic, Kvasiny, Czech Republic and other plants. Body styles: 5-door hatchback, 5-door estate, 2-door coupé utility (pick up), 2-door panel van. Layout: Front-engine, front-wheel-drive.
