Škoda Enyaq

Škoda · Czech Republic

2020–present

Abstract

The Škoda Enyaq is a mid-size crossover SUV (D), built November 2020–present in Czech Republic: Mladá Boleslav. Body styles: 5-door SUV, 5-door coupé SUV (Enyaq Coupe), 5-door panel van (Enyaq Cargo). Layout: 80X and RS: Dual-motor, all-wheel-drive. Designed by Omer Halilhodžić, Dalibor Pantůček, Sajdin Osmančević.
