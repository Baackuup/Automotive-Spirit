Fourth generation (B9, Typ 3Y; 2023) · 2023–present

Abstract

This generation of the Škoda Superb was built December 2023–present in Slovakia: Bratislava. Body styles: 5-door liftback, 5-door estate. Layout: Front-engine, front-wheel-drive, Front-engine, all-wheel-drive.
