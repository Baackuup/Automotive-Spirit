Kia K5

Kia · South Korea

Fifth generation (DL3; 2019) · 2019–present

Abstract

This generation of the Kia K5 was built 2019–present in South Korea: Hwaseong (Hwaseong Plant), United States: West Point, Georgia (KMMG), China: Yancheng (Yancheng Plant) and other plants. Body styles: 4-door sedan. Designed by Karim Habib.
