Kia Stinger

Kia · South Korea

2017–2023

Abstract

The Kia Stinger is a mid-size car (D), built October 2017 – April 2023 in South Korea: Gwangmyeong (Sohari Plant), Russia: Kaliningrad (Avtotor), Kazakhstan: Ust-Kamenogorsk (ASAV). Body styles: 5-door liftback/fastback sedan. Layout: FMR layout. Designed by Sang-hun Ahn under Peter Schreyer.
