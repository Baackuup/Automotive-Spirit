Kia EV9

Kia · South Korea

2023–present

Abstract

The Kia EV9 is a mid-size crossover SUV (E), built 2023–present in South Korea: Gwangmyeong, United States: West Point, Georgia (KMMG). Body styles: 5-door SUV. Layout: Rear-motor, rear-wheel-drive. Designed by Karim Habib (design chief).
