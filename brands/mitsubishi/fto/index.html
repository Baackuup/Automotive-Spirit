Mitsubishi FTO

Mitsubishi · Japan

1994–2000

Abstract

The Mitsubishi FTO is a sport compact, built 1994–2000 in Japan: Okazaki, Aichi (Nagoya Plant). Body styles: 2-door coupe. Layout: Front-engine, front-wheel-drive.
