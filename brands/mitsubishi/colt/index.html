Mitsubishi Colt

Mitsubishi · Japan

Colt 600, 800, 1000, 1100, 1200, 1500 (1962–1971) · 1965–1971

Abstract

This generation of the Mitsubishi Colt was built 1965–1971. Body styles: 2-door fastback, 3-door liftback, 4-door fastback, 3-door wagon/van, 2-door coupé utility/pickup. Layout: Front-engine, rear-wheel drive. Designed by Tsutomu Ieda, Munetaro Shigehiro, Ryu Kaibuchi. This generation has its own article: Mitsubishi Colt 800.
