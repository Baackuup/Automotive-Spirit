Mitsubishi Delica

Mitsubishi · Japan

Fifth generation (2007) · 2007–present

Abstract

This generation of the Mitsubishi Delica is a large MPV / Minivan (M), built January 2007 – present in Japan: Sakahogi, Gifu (Pajero Manufacturing Co., Ltd) (2007–2021), Okazaki, Aichi (Okazaki Plant) (2021–present). Body styles: 5-door van. Layout: Front-engine, front-wheel-drive. Designed by Akinori Nakanishi.
