Mitsubishi Colt 1000

Mitsubishi · Japan

Colt 1200 (series A23) · 1968–1970

Abstract

This generation of the Mitsubishi Colt 1000 was built May 1968–1970 in Japan: Okazaki, Aichi (Nagoya Plant), Malaysia: Tampoi, Johor (C&C). Body styles: 4-door sedan, 3-door wagon, 5-door wagon. Layout: Front-engine, rear-wheel drive.
