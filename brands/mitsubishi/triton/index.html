Mitsubishi Triton

Mitsubishi · Japan

Sixth generation (LC/MV; 2023) · 2023–present

Abstract

This generation of the Mitsubishi Triton was built 2023–present in Thailand: Laem Chabang (MMTh), Brazil: Catalão (HPE). Body styles: 2-door pickup, 4-door pickup. Designed by Norihiko Yoshimine.
