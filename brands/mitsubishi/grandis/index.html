Mitsubishi Grandis

Mitsubishi · Japan

2003–2011

Abstract

The Mitsubishi Grandis is a mid-size MPV, built 2003–2011 in Japan: Okazaki, Aichi (Nagoya plant), Thailand: Laem Chabang (MMTh). Body styles: 5-door wagon. Layout: Front-engine, front-wheel-drive. Designed by Olivier Boulay.
