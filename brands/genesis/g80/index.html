Genesis G80

Genesis · South Korea

Second generation (RG3; 2020) · 2020–present

Abstract

This generation of the Genesis G80 was built April 2020 – present in South Korea: Ulsan (Hyundai Motors Ulsan Plant). Layout: Front-engine, rear-wheel-drive. Designed by Luc Donckerwolke.
