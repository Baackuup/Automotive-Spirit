Mazda MPV

Mazda · Japan

Third generation (LY; 2006) · 2006–2016

Abstract

This generation of the Mazda MPV was built 2006–2016 in Japan: Hiroshima (Hiroshima Plant), China: Changchun (FAW Mazda). Body styles: 5-door van. Layout: Front-engine, front-wheel-drive.
