Mazda3

Mazda · Japan

Fourth generation (BP; 2019) · 2019–present

Abstract

This generation of the Mazda3 was built 2019–present in Japan: Hōfu, Yamaguchi Prefecture (Hōfu Plant), Mexico: Salamanca, Guanajuato (MMVO), Thailand: Rayong (AutoAlliance Thailand) and other plants. Designed by Yasutake Tsuchida (2016).
