Mazda Premacy

Mazda · Japan

Third generation (CW; 2010) · 2010–2018

Abstract

This generation of the Mazda Premacy was built July 2010 – March 2018 in Japan: Hiroshima (Hiroshima Plant), Taiwan: Zhongli (Ford Lio Ho). Body styles: 5-door minivan. Layout: Front-engine, front-wheel-drive.
