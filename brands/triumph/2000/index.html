Triumph 2000

Triumph · United Kingdom

Triumph 2000 Mk 2, 2.5 PI Mk 2, 2500TC and 2500S · 1969–2000

Abstract

This generation of the Triumph 2000 was built 1969–1977 (2000). Body styles: 4-door saloon, 5-door estate.
