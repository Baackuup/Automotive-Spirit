Audi Q3

Audi · Germany

Third generation (Typ FJ; 2025) · 2025–present

Abstract

This generation of the Audi Q3 was built July 2025 – present in Hungary: Gyor, Germany: Ingolstadt, (Audi AG Werk Ingolstadt) and other plants. Body styles: 5-door SUV, 5-door coupé SUV (Sportback). Designed by Ramon Sellers, Seulah Park.
