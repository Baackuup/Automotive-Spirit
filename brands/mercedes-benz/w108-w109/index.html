Mercedes-Benz W108/W109

Mercedes-Benz · Germany

1965–1972

Abstract

The Mercedes-Benz W108/W109 is a full-size luxury car (F), built September 1965 – November 1972 in West Germany: Stuttgart. Body styles: 4-door saloon. Layout: FR layout. Designed by Paul Bracq, Friedrich Geiger (1963).
