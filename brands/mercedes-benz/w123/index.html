Mercedes-Benz W123

Mercedes-Benz · Germany

1975–1986

Abstract

The Mercedes-Benz W123 is an executive car (E), built November 1975 – January 1986 in West Germany: Sindelfingen; Stuttgart; Bremen, South Africa: East London (sedan only), China: Changchun (FAW: 1987–1988) and other plants. Body styles: 4-door saloon (W123), 2-door coupé (C123), 5-door estate (S123), 4-door limousine (V123). Layout: FR layout. Designed by Friedrich Geiger, Bruno Sacco (Saloon & coupe: 1973, T-Model: 1975).
