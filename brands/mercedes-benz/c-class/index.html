Mercedes-Benz C-Class

Mercedes-Benz · Germany

Fifth generation (W206; 2021) · 2021–present

Abstract

This generation of the Mercedes-Benz C-Class is a compact executive car (D), built March 2021–present in Germany: Bremen, South Africa: East London, China: Beijing (Beijing Benz: V206 LWB sedan) and other plants. Body styles: 4-door sedan, 5-door estate. Layout: Front-engine, rear-wheel-drive or all-wheel-drive (4Matic). Designed by Balázs Filczer under Gorden Wagener.
