Mercedes-Benz G-Class

Mercedes-Benz · Germany

W463, second generation (2018–2024) · 2018–2024

Abstract

This generation of the Mercedes-Benz G-Class is a mid-size luxury SUV, built May 2018–March 2024 in Austria: Graz (Magna Steyr). Body styles: 5-door SUV. Layout: F4 (4MATIC). Designed by Balázs Filczer.
