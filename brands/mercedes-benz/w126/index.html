Mercedes-Benz W126

Mercedes-Benz · Germany

1979–1993

Abstract

The Mercedes-Benz W126 is a full-size luxury car (F), built December 1979 – October 1991 (saloon) in Germany: Sindelfingen, Malaysia: Johor Bahru (OASB), South Africa: East London. Body styles: 4-door saloon, 2-door coupé (C126). Layout: FR layout. Designed by Bruno Sacco, Werner Breitschwerdt (saloon: 1975, 1976; coupé: 1977).
