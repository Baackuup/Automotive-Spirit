Mercedes-Benz W124

Mercedes-Benz · Germany

1985–1997

Abstract

The Mercedes-Benz W124 is an executive car/Grand tourer (E/S), built Saloon: January 1985 – August 1995 in Germany: Bremen; Rastatt; Sindelfingen; Zuffenhausen, Poland: Karczew, South Africa: Eastern Cape and other plants. Body styles: 4-door saloon, 5-door estate, 2-door coupé, 2-door cabriolet, 6-door limousine. Layout: Front engine, rear-wheel drive / four-wheel drive. Designed by Joseph Gallitzendörfer and Peter Pfeiffer (initial design), Bruno Sacco (final design).
