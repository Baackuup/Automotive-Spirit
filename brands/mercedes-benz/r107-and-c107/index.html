Mercedes-Benz R107 and C107

Mercedes-Benz · Germany

1971–1989

Abstract

The Mercedes-Benz R107 and C107 is a sports car/Grand tourer, built April 1971 – March 1989 (SL) in West Germany: Sindelfingen, South Africa: East London (CKD). Body styles: two-door roadster, two-door coupé. Layout: FR layout. Designed by Joseph Gallitzendörfer; Friedrich Geiger (1968).
