Mercedes-Benz GLC Electric

Mercedes-Benz · Germany

2026–present

Abstract

The Mercedes-Benz GLC Electric is a compact luxury crossover SUV (D), built 2026–present in Germany: Bremen; Sindelfingen, China: Beijing (Beijing Benz for L models), India: Pune (Mercedes Benz India) and other plants. Body styles: 5-door SUV. Layout: Rear-motor, rear-wheel-drive (GLC 250, 300+). Designed by Matthias Schenker and Mayya Markova under Gorden Wagener.
