Mercedes-Benz GLE

Mercedes-Benz · Germany

Fourth generation (W167/C167; 2018) · 2018–present

Abstract

This generation of the Mercedes-Benz GLE was built October 2018–present in United States: Tuscaloosa, Alabama (MBUSI), Egypt: 6th of October City (EGA), India: Pune, Maharashtra (MBI) and other plants. Body styles: 5-door SUV, 5-door coupe SUV. Designed by Robert Lešnik, Balázs Filczer, Michael Frei.
