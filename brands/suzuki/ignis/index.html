Suzuki Ignis

Suzuki · Japan

Second generation (MF; 2016) · 2016–2026

Abstract

This generation of the Suzuki Ignis is a crossover city car (A-segment), built 2016–2026 in Japan: Makinohara, Shizuoka (Sagara Assembly Plant), India: Gurgaon (Maruti Suzuki). Body styles: 5-door crossover SUV. Layout: Front-engine, front-wheel-drive or four-wheel-drive "AllGrip". Designed by Yoshio Takeuchi, Takumi Arita and Yuta Watanabe (facelift).
