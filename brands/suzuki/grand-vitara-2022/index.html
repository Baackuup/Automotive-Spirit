Suzuki Grand Vitara

Suzuki · Japan

2022–present

Abstract

The Suzuki Grand Vitara is a subcompact crossover SUV, built August 2022 – present in India: Bidadi, Karnataka (TKM). Body styles: 5-door SUV. Layout: Front-engine, front-wheel-drive. Designed by Yumiko Imano and Alessandro Di Gregorio.
