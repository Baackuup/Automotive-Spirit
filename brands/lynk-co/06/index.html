Lynk & Co 06

Lynk & Co · China

2020–present

Abstract

The Lynk & Co 06 is a subcompact crossover SUV (B), built 2020–present in China: Chengdu, Sichuan (Longquanyi Plant). Body styles: 5-door SUV. Layout: Front-engine, front-wheel-drive. Designed by Jon Rådbrink, Alessia Ombrella, Ignacio Fernández Miño.
