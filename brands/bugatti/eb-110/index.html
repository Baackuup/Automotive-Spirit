Bugatti EB 110

Bugatti · France

1991–1995

Abstract

The Bugatti EB 110 is a sports car (S), built 1991–1995 in Italy: Modena, Campogalliano (Bugatti Automobili Campogalliano). Body styles: 2-door coupé. Layout: Rear mid-engine, four-wheel drive. Designed by Marcello Gandini (prototype styling), Giampaolo Benedini (production styling and interior), Paolo Stanzani, Tecnostile (prototype Chief Engineering).
