Maserati Coupé

Maserati · Italy

2001–2008

Abstract

The Maserati Coupé is a grand tourer (S), built Spyder: 2001–2007 in Italy: Modena. Body styles: 2-door 2+2 coupé, 2-door convertible. Layout: Front-engine, rear-wheel-drive. Designed by Giorgetto Giugiaro at Italdesign, Enrico Fumia (Interior), Frank Stephenson (GranSport).
