Maserati Biturbo

Maserati · Italy

1981–1994

Abstract

The Maserati Biturbo is a grand tourer, built 1981–1994 in Coupé/Saloon:, Italy: Modena (Maserati), Spyder: and other plants. Body styles: 2-door 2+2 coupé, 2-door, 2+3 coupé (228), 4-door saloon/sedan, 2-door convertible. Layout: Front-engine, rear-wheel-drive. Designed by Pierangelo Andreani (1977), Marcello Gandini (1988 and 1991 facelifts), Giuseppe Mittino at Zagato (Spyder).
