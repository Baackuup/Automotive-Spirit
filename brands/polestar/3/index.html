Polestar 3

Polestar · Sweden

2024–present

Abstract

The Polestar 3 is a mid-size luxury crossover SUV (E), built February 2024 – present in China: Chengdu, Sichuan (ZAMC, 2024–2026), United States: Ridgeville, South Carolina (South Carolina Factory). Body styles: 5-door SUV. Layout: Dual-motor, all-wheel-drive. Designed by Nahum Escobedo, Su-bum Lee under Maximilian Missoni.
