Plymouth Reliant

Plymouth · United States

1980–1988

Abstract

The Plymouth Reliant is a mid-size car, built October 1980 – December 1988 in United States: Newark, Delaware (Newark Assembly: sedans and wagons), United States: Detroit, Michigan (Jefferson Avenue Assembly), United States: Fenton, Missouri (Saint Louis Assembly: 2-door sedans only) and other plants. Body styles: 2-door coupe (1981–1989), 4-door sedan (1981–1989), 4-door wagon (1981–1988). Layout: Transverse front-engine, front-wheel drive.
