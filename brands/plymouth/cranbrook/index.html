Plymouth Cranbrook

Plymouth · United States

1951–1953

Abstract

The Plymouth Cranbrook is a full-size, built 1951–1953 in Lynch Road Assembly, Highland Park, MI, United States, San Leandro Assembly, San Leandro, California, Adelaide Assembly, South Australia. Body styles: 4-door sedan, 2-door coupe, 2-door hardtop coupe, 2-door convertible, 3-door wagon, 2-door coupe utility (Australia).
