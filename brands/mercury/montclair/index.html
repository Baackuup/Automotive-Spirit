Mercury Montclair

Mercury · United States

1955–1968

Abstract

The Mercury Montclair is a full-size, built 1955–1960 in Main plant, Wayne, Michigan, (Branch assembly) and other plants. Body styles: 2-door coupe, 4-door sedan, 2-door convertible, 2-door hardtop, 4-door hardtop. Layout: FR layout.
