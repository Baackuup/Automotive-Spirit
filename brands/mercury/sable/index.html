Mercury Sable

Mercury · United States

Fifth generation (2008–2009) · 2007–2009

Abstract

This generation of the Mercury Sable was built 2007–2009 in Chicago, Illinois. Body styles: 4-door sedan. Layout: Front engine, front-wheel drive / four-wheel drive. Designed by J Mays.
