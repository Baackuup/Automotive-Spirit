Mercury Marauder

Mercury · United States

Third generation (2003–2004) · 2002–2004

Abstract

This generation of the Mercury Marauder was built May 2002 – June 25, 2004 in Canada: St. Thomas, Ontario (St. Thomas Assembly Plant). Body styles: 4-door sedan.
