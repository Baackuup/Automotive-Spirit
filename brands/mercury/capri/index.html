Mercury Capri

Mercury · United States

Third generation (1991–1994) · 1991–1994

Abstract

This generation of the Mercury Capri was built 1991–1994 in Australia: Melbourne, Victoria (Broadmeadows). Body styles: 2-door roadster. Layout: FF layout.
