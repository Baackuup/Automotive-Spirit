Ford Kuga

Ford · United States

Third generation (C482; 2019) · 2019–present

Abstract

This generation of the Ford Kuga was built 2019–2025 (North America) in Spain: Valencia (Ford Valencia Plant), Germany: Cologne (Cologne Body & Assembly, Vignale trim), United States: Louisville, Kentucky (Louisville Assembly Plant, Escape) and other plants. Designed by Andrew Bazinski, Christopher Giroux, Nedzad Mujcinovic, Jordan Demkiw (facelift).
