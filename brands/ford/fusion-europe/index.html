Ford Fusion

Ford · United States

Second generation (2013) · 2012–2020

Abstract

This generation of the Ford Fusion was built 2012–2020 in Mexico: Hermosillo, Sonora (Hermosillo Stamping and Assembly), United States: Flat Rock, Michigan (Flat Rock Assembly Plant: 2013–2016). Designed by Chris Hamilton, Kemal Curić (2010), Dillon Blanski (2010, 2013).
