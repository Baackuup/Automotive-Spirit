Ford Bronco

Ford · United States

Sixth generation (U725; 2021) · 2021–present

Abstract

This generation of the Ford Bronco is a mid-size SUV, built June 2021 – present in United States: Wayne, Michigan (Michigan Assembly), China: Nanchang (Jiangling Ford Auto). Body styles: 2-door convertible SUV, 4-door convertible SUV. Designed by Paul Wraith (chief program designer, 2018), Robert Gelardi (design manager, 2018), Daniel Kangas (Bronco R, 2019).
