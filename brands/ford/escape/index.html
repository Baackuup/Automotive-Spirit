Ford Escape

Ford · United States

Fourth generation (2020) · 2019–2026

Abstract

This generation of the Ford Escape was built August 2019 – December 2025 (US) in United States: Louisville, Kentucky (Louisville Assembly Plant), China: Chongqing (Changan Ford), Spain: Valencia (Valencia Plant, Kuga) and other plants. Designed by Andrew Bazinski, Christopher Giroux, Nedzad Mujcinovic, Jordan Demkiw (facelift).
