Alfa Romeo Stelvio

Alfa Romeo · Italy

2016–present

Abstract

The Alfa Romeo Stelvio is a luxury crossover SUV (D-segment), built 2016–present in Italy: Piedimonte San Germano, Lazio (Cassino Plant). Body styles: 5-door SUV. Layout: Front-engine, all-wheel-drive/rear-wheel-drive (2.0 Turbo Petrol). Designed by Carmelo Giannone (Senior Exterior Designer), Centro Stile Alfa Romeo.
