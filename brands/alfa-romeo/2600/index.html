Alfa Romeo 2600

Alfa Romeo · Italy

1962–1968

Abstract

The Alfa Romeo 2600 is an executive car, built 1962–1968 in Italy: Portello Plant, Milan, South Africa: East London (Car Distributors Assembly: 1963–1968 – 2600 Berlina). Body styles: 4-door Berlina (sedan), 2-door Spider (convertible), 2-door Sprint (coupe). Layout: FR layout. Designed by Giorgetto Giugiaro at Bertone (Sprint), Ercole Spada at Zagato (SZ), Rodolfo Bonetto at Touring (Spider).
