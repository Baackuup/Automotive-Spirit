Alfa Romeo 145 and 146

Alfa Romeo · Italy

1994–2000

Abstract

The Alfa Romeo 145 and 146 is a small family car (C), built 1994–2000 (145) in Italy: Pomigliano d'Arco plant, Campania. Body styles: 3-door hatchback (145), 5-door hatchback (146). Layout: Longitudinal front-engine, front-wheel-drive (Boxer). Designed by Chris Bangle at Centro Stile Fiat (145), Centro Stile Alfa Romeo under Walter de Silva (146).
