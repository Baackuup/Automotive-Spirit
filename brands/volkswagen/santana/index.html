Volkswagen Santana

Volkswagen · Germany

Third generation (A05; 2012) · 2012–2022

Abstract

This generation of the Volkswagen Santana is a subcompact car, built 2012–2022 (China) in China: Yizheng, Jiangsu; Ürümqi, Xinjiang (SAIC-VW). Body styles: 4-door saloon, 5-door estate.
