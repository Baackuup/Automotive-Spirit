Volkswagen Beetle

Volkswagen · Germany

Volkswagen Beetle (A5) · 2011–2019

Abstract

This generation of the Volkswagen Beetle is a small family car (C), built July 2011 – July 2019 in Mexico: Puebla. Body styles: 3-door hatchback, 2-door cabriolet. Layout: Front-engine, front-wheel-drive. Designed by Walter de Silva, Marc Lichte.
