Volkswagen Up

Volkswagen · Germany

2011–2023

Abstract

The Volkswagen Up is a city car (A), built November-December 2011–2023 (Volkswagen up!) in Slovakia: Bratislava (Volkswagen Bratislava Plant), Brazil: Taubaté (Volkswagen do Brasil). Body styles: 3-door hatchback, 5-door hatchback. Layout: Front-engine, front-wheel-drive. Designed by Klaus Bischoff, Oliver Stefani and Marco Antonio Pavone.
