Volkswagen Golf Mk4

Volkswagen · Germany

1997–2014

Abstract

The Volkswagen Golf Mk4 is a compact car (C), built October 1997–2006 (Europe) in Germany: Wolfsburg, Germany: Zwickau (Volkswagen Zwickau-Mosel Plant), Brazil: São José dos Pinhais (Volkswagen do Brasil) and other plants. Body styles: 3-door hatchback, 5-door hatchback, 5-door station wagon. Layout: Front engine, front-wheel-drive / four-wheel-drive. Designed by Peter Schreyer, Hartmut Warkuß.
