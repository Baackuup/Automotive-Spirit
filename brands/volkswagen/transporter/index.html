Volkswagen Transporter

Volkswagen · Germany

Transporter (2024) · 2024–present

Abstract

This generation of the Volkswagen Transporter is a light commercial vehicle (M), built 2024–present in Turkey: Gölcük, Kocaeli (Ford Otosan). Body styles: 6-door van, 5-door minibus, 4-door crew cab. Layout: Front-engine, front-wheel-drive. This generation has its own article: Volkswagen Transporter (2024).
