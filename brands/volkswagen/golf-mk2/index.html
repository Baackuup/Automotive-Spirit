Volkswagen Golf Mk2

Volkswagen · Germany

1983–1992

Abstract

The Volkswagen Golf Mk2 is a small family car (C), built September 1983–1992 in Germany: Wolfsburg, Germany: Zwickau (Volkswagen Zwickau-Mosel Plant), Belgium: Brussels and other plants. Body styles: 3-door hatchback, 5-door hatchback. Layout: Front engine, front-wheel drive / four-wheel drive. Designed by Herbert Schäfer.
