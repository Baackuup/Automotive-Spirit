Volkswagen Golf Mk5

Volkswagen · Germany

2003–2009

Abstract

The Volkswagen Golf Mk5 is a small family car (C), built August 2003 – 2009 in Germany: Wolfsburg, South Africa: Uitenhage, Belgium: Brussels (Audi Brussels) and other plants. Body styles: 3-door hatchback, 5-door hatchback, 5-door estate/wagon. Layout: Front-engine, front-wheel-drive. Designed by Hartmut Warkuss, Peter Schreyer and Marc Lichte (hatchback), Murat Günak (estate).
