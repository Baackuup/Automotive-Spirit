BMW 1 Series

BMW · Germany

Fourth generation (F70; 2024) · 2024–present

Abstract

This generation of the BMW 1 Series is a subcompact executive car, built July 2024 – present in Germany: Leipzig (BMW Leipzig Plant). Body styles: 5-door hatchback. Layout: Front-engine, front-wheel-drive. Designed by José Casas, Jae-heon Lee, Ted Lee (exterior), Julius Laurien (interior). This generation has its own article: BMW 1 Series (F70).
