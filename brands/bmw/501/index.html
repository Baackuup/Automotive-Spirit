BMW 501

BMW · Germany

1952–1962

Abstract

The BMW 501 is a luxury car, built October 1952–1962. Body styles: 4-door sedan/saloon, 2-door cabriolet, 2-door coupé. Layout: FR layout. Designed by Peter Szymanowski.
