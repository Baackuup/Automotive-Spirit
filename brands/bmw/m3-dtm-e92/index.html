BMW M3 DTM (E92)

BMW · Germany

Deutsche Tourenwagen Masters (Touring Cars)

Abstract

The BMW M3 DTM (E92) is a racing car built for Deutsche Tourenwagen Masters (Touring Cars) by BMW. Debut: 2012 Hockenheimring 1 Deutsche Tourenwagen Masters round.
