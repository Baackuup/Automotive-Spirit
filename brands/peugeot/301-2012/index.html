Peugeot 301

Peugeot · France

Peugeot 301 (2012) · 2012–2021

Abstract

This generation of the Peugeot 301 is a subcompact car (B), built 2012–2021 in China: Wuhan (DPCA), Spain: Vigo (PSA Vigo Plant), Iran: Tehran (IKAP) and other plants. Body styles: 4-door saloon. Layout: Front-engine, front-wheel-drive.
