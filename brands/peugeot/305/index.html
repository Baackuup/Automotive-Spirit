Peugeot 305

Peugeot · France

1977–1993

Abstract

The Peugeot 305 is a small family car/Large family car (C/D), built 1977–1989 in France: Sochaux (Sochaux Plant), Chile: Los Andes (Automotores Franco Chilena S.A.), Morocco: Casablanca (Sopriam) and other plants. Body styles: 4-door saloon, 5-door estate, 3-door panel van. Layout: FF layout. Designed by Pininfarina.
