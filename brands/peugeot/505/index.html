Peugeot 505

Peugeot · France

1979–1997

Abstract

The Peugeot 505 is a large family car (D), built 1979–1992 (Europe) in France: Sochaux (Sochaux Plant), Argentina: Villa Bosch (Sevel), Australia: Heidelberg, Victoria (Renault Australia) and other plants. Body styles: 4-door saloon, 5-door estate. Layout: FR layout. Designed by Pininfarina and Peugeot.
