Peugeot 208

Peugeot · France

Second generation (P21; 2019) · 2019–present

Abstract

This generation of the Peugeot 208 was built 2019–present in Slovakia: Trnava (Trnava Plant), Spain: Zaragoza (Opel España de Automóviles), Morocco: Kenitra (Kenitra Plant) and other plants. Body styles: 5-door hatchback. Designed by Kevin Gonçalves (exterior), Yann Beurel (exterior), Eric Dejou (interior).
