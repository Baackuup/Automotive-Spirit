Peugeot 206

Peugeot · France

1998–present

Abstract

The Peugeot 206 is a supermini (B), built May 1998 – December 2012 (France) in France: Mulhouse (Mulhouse Plant: 3/5-door hatchback), France: Poissy (Poissy Plant: 206 SW), France: Cerizay (Heuliez: 206 CC) and other plants. Body styles: 3/5-door hatchback, 2-door coupé cabriolet (206 CC), 5-door estate (206 SW), 4-door saloon (206 SD). Layout: Front-engine, front-wheel-drive. Designed by Gérard Welter (206), Murat Günak (206 CC).
