Peugeot 508

Peugeot · France

Second generation (R83; 2018) · 2018–present

Abstract

This generation of the Peugeot 508 was built 2018–2025 in France: Mulhouse (Mulhouse Plant), China: Wuhan (DPCA), Iran: Semnan (FMC). Body styles: 5-door liftback, 5-door estate, 4-door saloon (508L). Designed by Giovanni Rizzo (exterior, liftback), Sylvain Henry (exterior, estate).
