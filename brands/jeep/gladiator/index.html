Jeep Gladiator

Jeep · United States

Jeep Gladiator (JT) · 2019–present

Abstract

This generation of the Jeep Gladiator is a mid-size pickup truck, built April 2019–present in United States: Toledo, Ohio (Toledo Complex). Body styles: 4-door pickup truck with removable roof and doors. Layout: Front-engine, four-wheel-drive. Designed by Taylor Langhals. This generation has its own article: Jeep Gladiator (JT).
