Jeep Cherokee

Jeep · United States

Sixth generation (KM; 2026) · 2025–present

Abstract

This generation of the Jeep Cherokee is a compact crossover SUV, built August 2025–present in Mexico: Toluca (Toluca Car Assembly). Body styles: 5-door SUV. Layout: Front-engine, four-wheel-drive. This generation has its own article: Jeep Cherokee (KM).
