Jeep CJ

Jeep · United States

1944–1986

Abstract

The Jeep CJ is a compact sport utility vehicle, built 1944–1986 in United States: Toledo, Ohio (Toledo Complex), United States: Maywood, California, Argentina: Santa Isabel (IKA) and other plants. Body styles: doorless / 2-door SUV, doorless / 2-door convertible, 2-door pickup truck. Layout: Front engine, rear-wheel drive / four-wheel drive.
