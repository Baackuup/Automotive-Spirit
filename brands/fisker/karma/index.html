Fisker Karma

Fisker · United States

2011–2012

Abstract

The Fisker Karma is a full-size luxury car, built 2011–2012 in Finland: Uusikaupunki (Valmet Automotive). Body styles: 4-door sedan. Layout: Front-engine, rear-wheel drive. Designed by Henrik Fisker.
