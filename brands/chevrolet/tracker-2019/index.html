Chevrolet Tracker

Chevrolet · United States

Chevrolet Tracker (2019) · 2019–present

Abstract

This generation of the Chevrolet Tracker is a subcompact crossover SUV, built 2019–present in China: Shenyang, Liaoning (SAIC-GM) (2019–2025), Brazil: São Caetano do Sul (GM Brasil), Argentina: Alvear, Santa Fe (GM Argentina, 2022–present) and other plants. Body styles: 5-door SUV. Layout: Front-engine, front-wheel-drive. Designed by Hao Huang, Zongheng Sun.
