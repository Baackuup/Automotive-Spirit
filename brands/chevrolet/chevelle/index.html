Chevrolet Chevelle

Chevrolet · United States

Third generation (1973–1977) · 1972–1977

Abstract

This generation of the Chevrolet Chevelle was built 1972–1977 in Arlington, Texas, United States (Arlington Assembly), Baltimore, Maryland, United States (Baltimore Assembly), Doraville, Georgia, United States (Doraville Assembly) and other plants. Body styles: 2-door coupe, 4-door sedan, 4-door station wagon.
