Chevrolet Monte Carlo

Chevrolet · United States

Sixth generation (2000–2007) · 1999–2007

Abstract

This generation of the Chevrolet Monte Carlo was built October 1999–2007 in Oshawa, Ontario, Canada. Body styles: 2-door coupe. Layout: Transverse front-engine, front-wheel drive. Designed by Wayne Cherry (1996).
