Chevrolet S-10 Blazer

Chevrolet · United States

Second generation (1995–2005) · 1994–2012

Abstract

This generation of the Chevrolet S-10 Blazer was built 1994–2005 (North America) in United States: Linden, New Jersey (Linden Assembly), Moraine, Ohio (Moraine Assembly), Brazil: São José dos Campos (GM Brazil) and other plants. Body styles: 2-door SUV, 4-door SUV.
