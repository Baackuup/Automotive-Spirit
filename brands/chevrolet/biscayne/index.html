Chevrolet Biscayne

Chevrolet · United States

Fifth generation (1971–1975) · 1971–1975

Abstract

This generation of the Chevrolet Biscayne was built 1971–1972 (US) in Janesville, Wisconsin (Janesville Assembly), Oshawa, Ontario, Canada (Oshawa Car Assembly). Body styles: 4-door sedan, 4-door station wagon (Canada, 1973-75).
