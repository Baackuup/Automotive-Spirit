Chevrolet Venture

Chevrolet · United States

1996–2005

Abstract

The Chevrolet Venture is a minivan, built July 1996 – June 24, 2005 in Doraville Assembly, Doraville, Georgia, United States, Shanghai, China (Buick GL8 and a 10-seater version of the Chevrolet Venture exclusive for the Philippines). Body styles: 3-door minivan, 4-door minivan. Layout: Transverse front-engine, front-wheel drive / all-wheel drive. Designed by Wayne Cherry (1993).
