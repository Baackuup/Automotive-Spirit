Chevrolet 400

Chevrolet · United States

1962–1974

Abstract

The Chevrolet 400 is an executive car, built 1962–1974 in Argentina: San Martín, Buenos Aires. Body styles: 4-door sedan. Layout: Front engine, rear-wheel drive.
