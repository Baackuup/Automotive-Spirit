Saab 92

Saab · Sweden

1949–1957

Abstract

The Saab 92 is a small family car (C), built 1949–1957 in Sweden: Trollhättan (Trollhättan Assembly). Body styles: 2-door coupé. Layout: Transverse front-engine, front-wheel drive. Designed by Sixten Sason.
