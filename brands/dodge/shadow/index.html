Dodge Shadow

Dodge · United States

1986–1994

Abstract

The Dodge Shadow is a compact/C-segment, built August 1986 – March 1994 in United States: Sterling Heights, Michigan (Sterling Heights Assembly), Mexico: Toluca, Mexico State (Toluca Car Assembly). Body styles: 2-door convertible (Shadow only), 3/5-door hatchback. Layout: Transverse front-engine, front-wheel drive.
