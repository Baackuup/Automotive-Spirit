Dodge 330

Dodge · United States

1962–1965

Abstract

The Dodge 330 is a full size, built 1962–1964 (until 1965 for Canada). Body styles: 2-door sedan, 4-door sedan, 4-door station wagon, 2-door hardtop (1962). Layout: FR. Designed by Virgil Exner.
