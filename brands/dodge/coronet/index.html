Dodge Coronet

Dodge · United States

Seventh generation (1975–1976) · 1975–1976

Abstract

This generation of the Dodge Coronet was built 1975–1976 in United States: Hamtramck, Michigan (Dodge Main Factory). Body styles: 4-door wagon (1975–1976), 4-door sedan (1975–1976), 2-door hardtop/coupe (1975 only).
