Dodge Viper

Dodge · United States

Fifth generation (VX I, 2013–2017) · 2012–2017

Abstract

This generation of the Dodge Viper is a sports car (S), built 2012–2017 in Conner Avenue Assembly, Detroit, Michigan, United States. Body styles: 2-door liftback coupé. Layout: Front mid-engine, rear-wheel-drive. Designed by Scott Krugger (2010). This generation has its own article: Dodge Viper (VX I).
