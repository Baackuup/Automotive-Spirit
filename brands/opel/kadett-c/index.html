Opel Kadett C

Opel · Germany

1973–1979

Abstract

The Opel Kadett C is a small family car (C), built 1973–1979 in West Germany: Bochum, Belgium: Antwerp. Body styles: "Limousine" (2/4-door saloon), "City" 3-door hatchback, "Caravan" (3-door estate), Coupé, "Aero" (targa-top cabriolet).
