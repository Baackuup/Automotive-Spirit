Alpine A460

Alpine · France

Le Mans Prototype 2

Abstract

The Alpine A460 is a racing car built for Le Mans Prototype 2, (LMP2) by Oreca. Debut: 2015 6 Hours of Silverstone.

The Alpine A460 shares its generations with the Oreca 05: those listed are of that common design.
