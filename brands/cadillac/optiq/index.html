Cadillac Optiq

Cadillac · United States

2023–present

Abstract

The Cadillac Optiq is a compact luxury crossover SUV, built 2024–present in China: Wuhan (SAIC-GM), Mexico: Ramos Arizpe (Ramos Arizpe Assembly). Body styles: 5-door SUV. Layout: Front-motor, front-wheel-drive (2023 - 2026). Designed by Jason Chen, Namsuk Lee, Dillon Blanski.
