Cadillac Seville

Cadillac · United States

Fifth generation (1998–2004) · 1997–2003

Abstract

This generation of the Cadillac Seville was built 1997–2003 in United States: Detroit/Hamtramck Assembly, Hamtramck, Michigan. Body styles: 4-door sedan. Layout: Transverse front-engine, front-wheel drive.
