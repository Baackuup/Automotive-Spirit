Cadillac ATS

Cadillac · United States

2012–2019

Abstract

The Cadillac ATS is a compact executive car, built July 2012 – July 2019 (United States) in United States: Lansing, Michigan (Lansing Grand River Assembly), China: Shanghai (SAIC-GM). Body styles: 4-door sedan, 2-door coupe. Layout: Front-engine, rear-wheel-drive. Designed by Bob Boniface (exterior), Eric Clough (interior).
