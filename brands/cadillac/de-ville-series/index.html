Cadillac de Ville series

Cadillac · United States

Eighth generation (2000–2005) · 1999–2005

Abstract

This generation of the Cadillac de Ville series was built August 1999–June 2005 in Detroit/Hamtramck Assembly, Hamtramck, Michigan, U.S.. Body styles: 4-door sedan. Layout: Transverse front-engine, front-wheel drive. Designed by Wayne Cherry.
