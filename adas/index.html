Assisted driving in the cars on sale

A first selection of 11 systems, highest level first. Levels follow SAE J3016: a system that steers and controls speed while the driver supervises is Level 2, one that lets the driver look away is Level 3. Select a model to open its sheet.

LevelModelSystemSource
L3BMW 7 Series G70Personal Pilot L3 and Highway Assistant option
Personal Pilot L3 drives in slow traffic up to 60 km/h on German motorways with separated carriageways, hands off and eyes off. Highway Assistant (Level 2) keeps speed, distance and lane up to 130 km/h hands off, and can change lane when the driver confirms by looking at the mirror.		BMW Group PressClub: Level 2 and Level 3 combined
L3Mercedes-Benz EQSDRIVE PILOT option
The same Level 3 system as the S-Class: up to 95 km/h on German motorways, under conditions, with lidar.		Mercedes-Benz Group: DRIVE PILOT, 95 km/h
L3Mercedes-Benz S-Class W223DRIVE PILOT option
Conditionally automated driving on German motorways: the car drives itself up to 95 km/h in normal traffic flow behind a vehicle ahead in the right lane, and the driver may look away. Uses lidar besides cameras and radar; turquoise lights show other road users that the system is active.		Mercedes-Benz Group: DRIVE PILOT, 95 km/h
L2Ford Mustang Mach-EBlueCruise option
Level 2 'hands-off, eyes-on' assistance on pre-mapped motorways (Blue Zones), approved by the European Commission for 15 European countries in 2024; the driver's eyes must stay on the road.		Ford Media: BlueCruise approved for 15 European countries
L2Nissan Qashqai J12ProPILOT Assist with Navi-link
Motorway assistance, eyes on and hands on, on roads separated by barriers; uses navigation data. Rated 'Very Good' in Euro NCAP's 2022 assisted-driving tests.		Nissan UK: Qashqai safety

Lane positioning assist, semi-automatic lane change and recommended speed adaptation, standard with the Drive Assist Pack on the GT Premium trim.		Stellantis Media UK: 3008 and 5008 GT Premium

Adaptive cruise control with Stop & Go (automatic gearbox) and lane keeping, plus a semi-automatic lane change on dual carriageways from 70 to 180 km/h.		Stellantis Media: Peugeot 308 digital innovations
L2Renault AustralActive Driver Assist
Level 2 assistance combining adaptive cruise control with Stop & Go and lane centring.		Renault: All-new Austral
L2Toyota Corolla E210Toyota Safety Sense 3.0
Standard suite: pre-collision system, lane tracing assist (keeps the car centred while radar cruise control is on), lane departure alert, road sign assist and proactive driving assist.		Toyota Pressroom: Corolla
L2Volkswagen Golf Mk8Travel Assist option
Assisted driving up to 210 km/h: the car steers, accelerates and brakes, combining adaptive cruise control and lane keeping; the driver keeps monitoring.		Volkswagen Newsroom: the new Golf
